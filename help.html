Support

How can we help?

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I book a ticket?

Open Transport, pick bus, train or air, then press Book on the trip you want.

How do I reserve a hotel?

Open Hotels, search by city and press Reserve on a hotel.

Why do I need my NID to register?

We use it to verify passenger identity for tickets.

How do I become a provider?

Use Become a provider, fill in the form, and wait for admin approval.
